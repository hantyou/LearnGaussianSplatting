What am I seeing?

Rendered mode uses projected Gaussian footprints and transparency. The balls are constant-density contours of those same primitives: their semi-axis lengths are σ, 2σ, or 3σ. Gaussians continue beyond these contours.

Real scenes are pretrained examples. The miniature street is a controlled image-fitting experiment. Neither ellipsoid shape nor opacity is automatically a probability of physical occupancy.

Large scenes are sampled in diagnostic modes. Full rendering uses all loaded Gaussians. Scene units are not necessarily meters.

Renderer: Spark + Three.js. Real samples: cakewalk/splat-data. See the local viewer guide for provenance and limitations.
